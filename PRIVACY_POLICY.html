Privacy Policy

Expenses Tracker · DerAK Soft

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Expenses Tracker ("the app") is developed by DerAK Soft ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

1. Data Storage

Expenses Tracker is a fully offline app. All data you enter — projects, transactions, categories, and receipt photos — is stored locally on your device only. We do not operate any server or backend, and your financial data is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app itself.

You are in full control of this data: you can export it (as CSV or a backup archive), restore it, or permanently delete it at any time from within the app's Settings.

2. Camera and Photo Permissions

The app requests camera and photo library access solely so you can attach a receipt photo to a transaction. These images are saved only in the app's local storage on your device and are never uploaded anywhere, unless you explicitly choose to share or back up your data yourself (e.g. via the Share/Export feature, which uses your device's own share sheet).

3. Advertising

The app shows banner advertisements served by Google AdMob to support free use of the app. To deliver and personalize ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process technical data such as your device's advertising identifier, general device information, and approximate location. This data collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and, for the consent flow shown to users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, its User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent mechanism.

You can permanently disable ads (and the associated ad-request data collection) at any time by purchasing the one-time "Remove Ads" option in Settings.

4. In-App Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely through Google Play's Billing system. We do not receive, see, or store any payment or card information — that is handled directly between you and Google Play.

5. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and the app does not transmit to us, any of the following: account credentials, analytics/usage tracking, crash reports, contact lists, or location data (beyond what the ad SDK described in Section 3 may independently collect for ad delivery).

6. Data Deletion

Because all data lives on your device, uninstalling the app or using Settings → Clear All Data permanently deletes it. There is no remote copy for us to delete, since none exists.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to: deraksoft@gmail.com
